The prototype measures savings on one supply chain. These calculators scale them up, with every assumption visible and adjustable, so you can judge the potential for yourself rather than take a headline number on trust.

Beyond the numbers

Who benefits, and how it scales

Hardware buyers

Cloud providers and OEMs get verified, per-lot Scope 3 data with a data-quality grade and carbon at risk, instead of averages.

Honest suppliers

Fabs with genuinely clean power and good abatement can prove it, with metered evidence and an on-chain trust score, without revealing trade secrets.

AI teams

Every training run and inference service gets a certificate with energy, water and its share of hardware carbon, plus a scheduler that cuts both.

Communities and regulators

Water use is weighted by local scarcity, and anyone can verify claims directly from the blockchain.

Recyclers

Recovered metals are rewarded with CIRC tokens, linking end of life back to the chip's passport.

Adoption path

No new reporting burden: suppliers upload existing reports. Lot-level passports cost a fraction of a cent on an L2, and passports nest from chiplet to server.
