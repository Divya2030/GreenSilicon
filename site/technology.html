Smart contracts

Five contracts, each enforcing one guarantee

ContractStandardWhat it guarantees
GreenSiliconPassportERC-721, EIP-712, AccessControl, MerkleProofOnly role-holding suppliers can attest their own stage, in lifecycle order, with replay protection. Component carbon is allocated once (unit-capped). Offsets can never exceed the footprint. Any single confidential field can be proven against a salted Merkle root. On-chain supplier trust score.
TelemetryRegistryEIP-712, MerkleProofMeters sign their own batches; batches must be contiguous in time, so outages cannot be skipped or backfilled; any reading is provable.
ComputeLedgerERC-721AI-job certificates with embodied carbon amortised from the chip passport on-chain, capped at rated life; each unit deployed once; reuse and incremental savings derived from prior certificates, so they cannot be inflated.
CarbonCreditRegistryERC-1155A registry serial can be tokenised once; retirement burns credits and binds them to a specific chip lot.
CircularityTokenERC-20Minted only by the passport contract when a recycler records verified recovery (1 CIRC = 1 kg).
AI

AI that is useful, and kept honest

Extraction

Claude reads supplier PDFs and returns a schema-validated disclosure with evidence quotes and gaps. Documents are treated as untrusted data.

Hybrid audit

Physics, the electricity floor, IoT meters and an IsolationForest score each record. Claude writes findings, but the final verdict is the stricter of rules and LLM, so it cannot be talked out of a physics violation.

Efficient by design

A content-addressed cache means identical documents, audits or questions never reach the model twice. Server-side refusal fallbacks keep the pipeline robust, and offline fallbacks keep the demo working.
